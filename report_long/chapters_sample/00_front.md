# Abstract

Using two waves of the Taiwan i-Generation Panel Study (TIGPS; Grade 7 in 2023 and Grade 8 in 2024; linked *n* = 8,319 students in 178 schools), we examined whether late bedtimes predict depressive symptoms one year later. A later Grade 7 bedtime predicted higher Grade 8 depressive symptoms (β = 0.065, 95% CI [0.031, 0.096]), whereas the reverse path was near zero (β = 0.003).
